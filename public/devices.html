Anduril reception notes

Android 0.8.5 and Desktop 0.6.4 recognize two verified Anduril MAC allocations in received Wi-Fi observations. Desktop can also match an observed ARP sender on your selected local network. These are local manufacturer hints with time and signal information when available; model and operator remain unknown.

Anduril Industries · IEEE MA-M
E8:B4:70:C… — exact 28-bit allocation
Anduril Imaging · IEEE MA-S
8C:1F:64:76:3… — exact 36-bit allocation

These hints stay on the receiver and do not create map sightings. Third-party radios, changed addresses, and non-Wi-Fi links may not match. Remote ID and other supported decoders continue independently. SDR spectrum shows RF energy; it cannot identify these products on frequency alone.
