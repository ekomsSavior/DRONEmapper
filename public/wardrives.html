Upload a log

  1. Sign in and open Wardrive logs & upload tokens.
  2. Name your uploader, confirm sharing and create a token. Save it privately.
  3. Choose a completed WiGLE CSV export, check its timezone and preview matching observations.
  4. Upload. Accepted observations appear under Possible signals; observations over two weeks old appear under Ghosts.

Select the whole CSV, up to 256 MiB. The browser reads it in chunks, filters locally and uploads matching rows in batches. Keep the page open to watch progress or cancel. If interrupted, retry with the same token; completed batches will not count twice.

The first supported matches are WiFi CAM and Tello-style camera-link names. A name match is an unconfirmed signal near the receiver. Other networks can use the same names. Ordinary Wi-Fi, Bluetooth devices and OUI-only matches are skipped. This log importer does not decode Remote ID.

Collect without internet

The Android app 0.7.1 adds Saved wardrives. Start listening, record locally, then finish the trip. At home, open the trip to upload its map reports or export WiGLE CSV. Stopping or restarting the app keeps the saved trip. Offline recording instructions ↗

Automatic uploads from a folder

Install Python 3.10+ and download the uploader above. On Windows, use py -3 instead of python3.

python3 wardrive-upload.py --save-token my-upload-token.txt
python3 wardrive-upload.py --watch ./completed-exports --token-file my-upload-token.txt --consent

Place completed exports in that folder and leave the terminal running. The uploader waits for files to stop changing, filters unrelated rows locally, uploads batches and skips completed files. Ctrl+C stops it. Keep the token file private and outside shared folders. For local-time exports use the capture-date offset, such as --timezone=-07:00.

Connect another app

Apps that support a custom upload destination can send WiGLE CSV to https://dronemapper.churchofmalware.org/v1/wardrives using a DRONEmapper bearer token and a timezone header. The API guide covers raw CSV, multipart uploads, responses and limits.

Biscuit Manager currently shows named upload destinations in the tester's app. A DRONEmapper token cannot add a destination by itself. Use a WiGLE CSV export here; direct auto-upload from Manager needs support in that app.

Your contribution stays under your control

Raw logs, network names and MAC addresses are not retained. Matching signals use a rounded 0.01° receiver area and the log's original FirstSeen timestamp. FirstSeen may predate the row's GPS fix, so the area is not an exact device position. Imports never count as live receivers. Repeated uploads do not inflate sightings. Remove the uploader in your account to revoke its token and remove its reports.
