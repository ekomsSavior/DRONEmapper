CHURCH OF MALWARE / SAVIORSEC

Police drone flight history.

Explore published missions and official agency flight dashboards. Publication schedules vary; a recorded mission shows past activity.

San Francisco flight logs are imported automatically, with an hourly check for new monthly publications. Each mapped entry preserves its flight date, reported duration and broad mission category. Entries without published coordinates are omitted.

Choose Public safety and History on the world map to explore imported records. The portals below open the agency’s own records; a link here does not mean every flight has been imported.
