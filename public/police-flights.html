Automatic flight coverage

Loading department coverage…

We check public flight feeds every 5–60 minutes. Agencies control publication delays and which flights they release. Large dashboards show their most recent 5,000 published rows; Tampa follows its last-30-days publication window. Duplicate rows are combined, and records with invalid dates, missing locations or conflicting flight details are omitted.
