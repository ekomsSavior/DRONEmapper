Devices → platforms → integrations

This library maps published architecture, not live devices. Each entry includes its evidence, access requirements and what remains unknown. Public flight feeds and scanner observations remain on the world map with their own timestamps.

Desktop’s optional local DNS observations can identify a domain lookup, including an ordinary website visit. They do not reveal encrypted messages, identify a nearby robot or create a map location.

Make contributed data useful

Keep the original observation time, device identifier, units and location source. An indoor X/Y position needs its coordinate frame and a verified geographic reference; an upload timestamp does not make an old record live.

Already have a supported wardrive file? Upload wardrive logs. Other vendor exports need a matching importer before they can populate DRONEmapper. Keep account tokens and private video out of shared files.

Communications terms
MQTT
Messages pass through a broker using topics. It is shared by many kinds of devices, not a drone identifier.
REST API
An application requests specific resources from a service. Public documentation does not mean public access to customer records.
Webhooks and streaming
Services deliver events or updates. Their timestamps and delivery delays matter when deciding whether a record is current.
Local / onboard
An interface on a robot, controller or local network. It may work without a cloud service.
